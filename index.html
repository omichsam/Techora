Our Story

Who We Are

Techora Solutions Ltd is a leading supplier and key distributor of high-quality equipment, spare parts, laboratory instruments, and industrial machinery serving Kenya's energy sector and beyond.

We partner with energy parastatals, government institutions, and private companies to deliver reliable, certified, and competitively priced solutions that keep critical infrastructure running smoothly. Our reach spans renewable energy, oil & gas, mining, aviation, marine & rail, laboratory, healthcare, telecoms, and industrial machinery.

With deep expertise and a commitment to excellence, Techora Solutions stands as the preferred procurement partner across East Africa.
